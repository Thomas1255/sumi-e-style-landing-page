# Continuous landscape — approval study

Status: awaiting user review. The live page, deployed assets, video timeline and animations have not been changed.

Generated using the built-in ImageGen tool. No Higgsfield generation or video credits were used in this iteration.

The user requested a single landscape with gates, a village, towers and varied terrain, divided into four backgrounds. This study replaces the earlier proposal for independent landscapes. It uses one continuous river and traditional shifting scroll perspective; viewpoints are painted approximations for the flock directions rather than a geometric 3D camera model.

Journey runs from the bottom of the master to its top:

1. Lotus wetlands and shrine gates — rear-follow flight.
2. Village, timber watchtower, bridge and rice terraces — river bends toward upper-left.
3. Bamboo, autumn groves, bridge and temple/pagoda — river bends toward upper-right.
4. Mountain gorge, waterfalls and remote temple — higher elevated view.

The four PNG sections are direct, unretouched crops of master.png. They join edge-to-edge; no separate generation, recoloring or rescaling was applied. crop-record.json records exact bounds and pixel validation. The 2x2 review sheet is a labeled layout of those sections.

## Master prompt

Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: ONE SINGLE CONTINUOUS VERY TALL VERTICAL LANDSCAPE PAINTING, aspect ratio exactly 4:9 (width:height, equivalent to 16:36), ideally 2048 x 4608. This master painting will later be cut into FOUR equal horizontal 16:9 website background sections. Generate ONE unified full-bleed piece, NOT four panels, NOT a collage, NO dividing lines, NO frames and NO labels.
Primary request: A richly varied Japanese river journey in a flat TWO-DIMENSIONAL NEO-TRADITIONAL SUMI-E AND TRANSPARENT WATERCOLOR picture-scroll aesthetic. A single winding pale celadon river links the entire height, through wetlands, gates, a village, towers, farmland, bamboo, autumn groves, and a high mountain gorge. The composition MUST remain continuous across every horizontal line. No skyline or blank sky band inside the painting. Warm ivory washi paper, confident dry charcoal brush strokes, pooled gray ink, soft pigment bleeds, simplified graphic shapes, restrained jade/ochre/rust red/blue-gray colors, clouds as unpainted-paper ribbons. Expressive painting, not photorealistic or 3D. Plenty of varied places to discover, not one repeated mountain texture.
Journey order is BOTTOM TO TOP; describe all four quarters but do not separate them:
BOTTOM QUARTER (the first flock, rear-follow): flying forward and down over broad lotus wetlands, reed islets, a small vermilion torii by a shore path, pale sandy banks and low pine hills. The open river rises from bottom-center toward the center near the top of this quarter. Lotus leaves are simple ink/watercolor discs. Draw terrain from above enough to see the tops of islands and canopies, but with a forward-looking sense of travel.
LOWER MIDDLE QUARTER (second flock, facing upper-left): the SAME river bends unmistakably AWAY TOWARD THE UPPER LEFT, from lower-right to upper-left across this area, viewed diagonally down from behind/right. Golden rice terraces, an inhabited-looking traditional village of clustered ivory houses with charcoal tiled roofs, cherry blossoms, a small arched bridge, a vermilion shrine gate and one slender timber watchtower. Roads connect the village to the gate and bridge. The route and river continue naturally through this quarter's edges.
UPPER MIDDLE QUARTER (third flock, facing upper-right): that SAME continuous river bends back AWAY TOWARD THE UPPER RIGHT from lower-left toward upper-right, viewed diagonally down from behind/left. Bamboo and dark pine forest, broad rusty-red maple washes, a red footbridge, stone path, temple courtyard and a graceful multi-tier pagoda tower on a rocky ledge. Mix wooded banks, small clearings and rocky river shelves rather than repeating the farmland. Continue the identical river and landforms across adjoining quarters.
TOP QUARTER (fourth flock, high rear/overhead): the route climbs into a more expansive mountain gorge with STEEP DOWNWARD AERIAL viewing: visible tops of ridges and pine crowns, river far beneath, white waterfalls down ink cliffs, a remote temple tower and low cloud ribbons weaving between ridges. The same river continues from the section below toward top-center. No horizon and no sky strip. Suggest height through tonal overlap and pale mist, not 3D shading.
Continuity: Use the traditional Japanese painted-scroll idea of a gently shifting viewpoint as the eye follows the river, while the artwork remains one coherent 2D landscape. Rivers, paths, slopes and vegetation must continue exactly across the imaginary 25%, 50%, and 75% horizontal crop boundaries. No abrupt biomes, scene borders, boxed sections or separate horizons. Keep the central 70% of the width useful throughout so later responsive crops retain the route. Include gates, a real village, towers, bridges, varied landforms and seasonal trees at varied scales, but no dense micro-engraving.
Output: ONE unlettered vertical master painting. Landscape ONLY, without cranes or other birds, without animals, without humans, no silhouettes in the sky, no UI, no captions, no text, no stamps or watermark. No photographic realism, CGI, game map, isometric diorama, plastic objects or 3D render. Flat painted ink and watercolor on washi.

Scene 1 — LOTUS WETLANDS, matching the rear-follow flock.

Maintain serene airy spring greens. This should feel like flying after the rear-facing cranes into the open valley, with a matching forward travel axis.
