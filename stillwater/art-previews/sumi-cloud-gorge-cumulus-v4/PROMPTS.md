# Gorge sky — cumulus clouds

Status: approved and implemented on 2026-10-29. Six transparent PNGs for the final cloud transition only; exact generation prompts preserved below.

Built-in ImageGen was used. The first layer was created without image inputs. The other five used the first layer only as a medium/palette reference, with a different original silhouette requested for every painting. Generated originals are preserved unchanged.

## left-rear.png

left / rear — High rounded billows with drifting horizontal vapor

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: one independent transparent PNG cloud layer for a neo-traditional Japanese sumi-e and watercolor website transition.
Primary request: paint actual airy CUMULUS CLOUDS, recognizable sky vapor with broad rounded billowing volumes and pale pooled shadows. A new original painting, not ocean waves or decorative wave motifs.
Subject and medium: hand-painted 2D Japanese ink wash and watercolor, luminous warm ivory cloud interiors, extremely restrained pale cool gray and a trace of celadon in the soft interior shadow pockets. Fine crisp dry-brush detail in the painted body, softer translucent vapor at the outer edges. Calm, buoyant, weightless atmosphere. Irregular large and small rounded lobes, not a row of identical scallops. Gentle diffuse shading; no dark outlines, dark stripes, or charcoal cliff faces.
Layer LEFT / REAR: wide landscape canvas. A loose, distant cloud group enters from beyond the LEFT edge. One large soft rounded puff sits in the upper-left, with two smaller unequal billows drifting horizontally toward the middle-right. Its lower edge dissolves into a few thin horizontal vapor wisps. The upper-right, far-right and lower-right are mostly genuine transparent alpha. Keep the group light and spacious, and make it an unmistakable bank of fluffy sky clouds. Native left-hand composition, never mirror a right cloud.
Output: one PNG with actual transparent alpha around the cloud; partly transparent vapor fringes and a substantial pale ivory painted body. Maximum clear brush detail, clean anti-aliased edges, no painted pixel blocks or global blur. No rectangular paper background, checkerboard, landscape, sea, water, mountains, buildings, birds, text, seals or borders. No breaking crests, curling hooks, spiral waves, foam, spray, waterfalls, vertical streaks or torn descending curtains. One cloud layer only; no contact sheet.
```
