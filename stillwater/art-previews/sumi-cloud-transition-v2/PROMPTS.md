# Layered sumi-e cloud studies — v2

Status: artwork preview awaiting user approval. The site's transition is unchanged.

This revision replaces the proposed two flat cloud banks with six separately generated transparent PNGs. Each side has a rear, middle and foreground plane. These are independent animation assets, not flattened layers inside one image.

All six were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. Only the approved landscape was supplied as a palette and medium reference; no left-hand cloud was supplied as the reference for its right-hand counterpart. Source PNG alpha is preserved, with no flipping, recoloring, cutting or other processing.

| Depth | Left silhouette | Right silhouette | Proposed movement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rear | Long horizontal wisps | Rising diagonal plumes | Slow, enters first |
| Middle | Broad uneven domes | Hooked shelves and receding waist | Medium, conceals the cut |
| Foreground | Low billow with projecting tongues | High arch with hanging folds | Fast, overlaps the other planes |

The opacity, overlap, viewport fitting, scroll timing and speed differences must be visually tuned after artwork approval. The suggested numeric speed multipliers in the JSON record are design intentions, not a working animation.

The previous v1 artwork is preserved in the adjacent folder.

## left-01-rear-wisps.png

left / rear — Long high streamers and a low trailing veil

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: ONE independently movable transparent PNG cloud layer for a sumi-e landscape website transition.
Reference image: the existing landscape is ONLY a palette and painterly-medium reference. Do not reproduce scenery.
Style: neo-traditional Japanese sumi-e and watercolor, warm ivory/cloud-white, subtle celadon, charcoal-gray ink diluted into soft washes. Express cloud volume through overlapping lobes, luminous faces and gentle ink-wash shadows underneath; retain a hand-painted 2D scroll illustration aesthetic. Feathered dry-brush fringes, graceful organic shapes, selective broken ink strokes. Actual alpha transparency around the cloud, including fine semi-transparent mist edges. No rectangular paper or solid background.
Critical: This is a SEPARATE animation layer, not a composite of all depth planes, not a sprite sheet. Do not copy, flip or mirror any cloud image. Paint an original asymmetric silhouette and original brush patterns. No repeating wallpaper motifs, uniform rows, obvious spiral stamps, dark uniform outlines, 3D rendering, photographic fog, cartoon bubbles, scenery, birds, mountains, sun, text, seals, watermarks, frames, or painted checkerboard.
Layer: LEFT REAR / distant, slowest.
Composition: wide landscape canvas, one elongated airy bank stretched horizontally. Origin is the LEFT edge. Several long unequal brush-swept mist streamers reach toward the RIGHT around the upper-middle of the canvas, with a thin low trailing veil. A delicate high crest is near the left third; much of the lower-right is empty transparency. One coherent flowing bank, not scattered tiny cloud icons.
Shape language: slender tapered ribbons and soft elongated lenticular cloud forms. NO circular scroll curls, NO repeated scallops. Pale cool gray/celadon shadow staining only underneath the upper ribbons, subtle cream-white soft bodies. Lightest, most distant of the three planes. Leave large transparent margins top and bottom so this can float over other cloud layers.
```
