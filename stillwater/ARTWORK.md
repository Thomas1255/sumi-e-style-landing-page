# Stillwater artwork

The current scroll-driven crane experience uses the new panorama and transparent crane atlas documented in [FLIGHT-ARTWORK.md](FLIGHT-ARTWORK.md). The earlier pond paintings below are retained as project assets.

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool. The four final paintings are saved as optimized WebP assets in `dist/assets/`: `pond-scroll.webp`, `frog.webp`, `carp.webp`, and `goldfish.webp`. The original generated PNGs remain in the tool's generated-images directory. No external imagery or stock photos are used.

## Hero: pond-scroll.webp

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: full-bleed landscape hero artwork for an elegant art website, not a website mockup. Create a museum-quality Neo-Traditional Japanese sumi-e ink and watercolor pond landscape painted on warm ivory handmade washi paper, wide landscape composition, approximately 3:2. The LEFT 40 percent is almost entirely empty warm ivory parchment with only very faint pale blue-gray mist, intentionally reserved for website text. The artwork is concentrated on the RIGHT 60 percent and lower edge. Two anatomically elegant red-crowned white cranes stand in shallow still water on the right, one tall with a long curving neck and one bending to look at the water, black wing feathers rendered with rich dry-brush ink. A small green frog sits visibly on a mossy river stone near the lower right. A dark bronze common carp with subtle scales swims beneath the surface near the center bottom. Two small rich vermilion goldfish with flowing fins swim in the pale blue-green water lower middle. Sparse lotus leaves and delicate reeds frame the lower edges. A windswept black pine branch enters the upper right corner. Far behind the cranes, soft blue-gray mountains disappear into mist, and a pale faded vermilion sun sits in the upper right. Organic watercolor blooms, ink bleed, delicate pigment granulation, irregular feathered paint edges fading naturally into warm ivory #eee8da paper, exceptional fine art details and strong elegant composition with ample negative space. Restrained palette: sumi charcoal, dusty blue, celadon green, warm parchment, small cinnabar accents. Quiet, poetic, refined, dreamlike yet animal anatomy realistic. The image must have absolutely NO text, NO lettering, NO calligraphy, NO signature, NO stamp, NO border, NO UI. Avoid koi markings: the carp is a natural dark bronze common carp, only the goldfish are orange-red.

## Chapter paintings

Each chapter painting used the following prompt, with its subject paragraph inserted after “no typography.”

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: fine art chapter illustration for a Japanese watercolor art landing page. Create one elegant landscape 3:2 painting, no typography. [SUBJECT] Neo-Traditional sumi-e and watercolor on warm ivory handmade washi paper, organic pigment bleeds, delicate granulation, soft irregular watercolor edges fading into creamy parchment at the outer edges. Museum-quality natural-history painting combined with poetic Japanese scroll composition. Restrained palette of charcoal ink, celadon green, dusty blue, warm parchment and cinnabar. Detailed yet loose gestural brushwork, generous negative space, quiet meditative atmosphere. Same cohesive aesthetic as a traditional misty pond scroll. Absolutely no text, no calligraphy, no signature, no seal, no border, no UI, no photoreal photography.

### Frog: frog.webp

A close-up of a beautiful small green Japanese tree frog sitting on a mossy dark river stone at the edge of a lily pond. One pale pink lotus bloom and graceful reeds, quiet celadon water with delicate concentric ripples, a few golden grasses. Frog occupies center-right, recognizable expressive anatomy.

### Common carp: carp.webp

Two natural bronze and charcoal common carp, NOT colorful ornamental koi, swimming through clear smoky blue-green pond water among long flowing aquatic grasses. Top-down slightly oblique view, beautifully articulated fish scales and strong flowing dark tails. A few thin golden sunlit reflections, graceful curved composition.

### Goldfish: goldfish.webp

Three elegant vivid cinnabar vermilion goldfish with translucent long flowing fantails, swimming in pale celadon and dusty blue still water. A few dark lotus leaves frame one corner. Beautiful realistic anatomy, fine delicate fins like flowing silk, tiny scattered golden flecks and subtle circular ripples.
