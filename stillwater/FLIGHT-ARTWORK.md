# Flight artwork and animation

The page uses a continuous canvas animation driven only by scroll position. Scrolling forward advances the flight; scrolling backward reproduces the same frame. Stopping the scroll holds the animation. No Higgsfield video-generation credits were spent.

The panorama and transparent crane parts were created with the built-in ImageGen tool. The final project assets are `dist/assets/flight-panorama.webp` and `dist/assets/crane-atlas.webp`. Original generated PNGs remain in the tool's generated-images directory. The canvas uses the body and two separately painted wings from the atlas; no video service is needed at runtime.

## Landscape prompt

Produce a 3:1 ultra-wide panorama, ideally 3072 by 1024 pixels. A continuous Neo-Traditional Japanese sumi-e ink and watercolor landscape on warm cream ivory handmade washi paper. At far left, a calm celadon pond with lotus leaves, reed beds, distant low hills and a tiny dark wooden bridge. In the middle, layered tall blue-gray misty mountains, a winding river, delicate pine forests and a small vermilion torii gate on the riverbank. At far right, dramatic charcoal sumi-e pine branches frame a rocky waterfall and distant pagoda fading into pale golden mist. The upper 45 percent of the whole painting is spacious pale ivory blue-gray atmospheric sky with a small faded vermilion sun near the middle, to give a flying bird room to travel. The bottom 55 percent contains the beautifully detailed landscape. Dreamlike yet sophisticated traditional naturalistic ink drawing, painterly watercolor bleeds, delicate pigment granulation, organic irregular brush edges, museum-quality hand-painted Japanese horizontal scroll. Restrained muted celadon, dusty slate blue, warm ivory parchment, charcoal black, and small cinnabar accents. Continuous landscape composition with no subdivisions, no panels, no framing, no horizon discontinuities. Absolutely NO birds or animals anywhere, NO cranes, NO text, NO calligraphy, NO signature, NO stamps, NO UI. This must be a painting, NOT a website mockup. Quiet, expansive, poetic, cinematic.

## Crane atlas prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: a transparent 2D animation art atlas, 2048x2048 square, containing exactly THREE isolated watercolor crane body parts for an animated Japanese art website. All background must be truly transparent alpha. Style: refined realistic Neo-Traditional Japanese sumi-e and watercolor, detailed soft naturalistic white and ivory feathers, rich ink-black flight feathers, delicate pigment granulation and feather fraying, museum-quality fine art. NOT vector art, NOT flat cartoon, NOT origami. Layout is essential: TOP HALF of the square contains ONE horizontal flying red-crowned crane BODY ONLY, facing RIGHT, long straight neck extended to the right and long thin legs trailing far to the left, small vermilion crown, fine long pointed beak. This body must have NO WINGS at all (wings are separate below), but includes white feathered torso, neck, head, tail and trailing legs. The body spans most of the width, centered vertically in the top half, with generous transparent margin around it. BOTTOM LEFT QUARTER contains exactly ONE separate fully extended crane wing, pointing UP and LEFT, with its shoulder joint at the BOTTOM RIGHT of that quarter. Feathers form a beautiful broad tapered natural wing, mostly creamy white inner coverts with black primary feathers along outer edge; the full wing and all feather tips must fit in the quarter with transparent margin. BOTTOM RIGHT QUARTER contains exactly ONE matching separate fully extended crane wing pointing UP and RIGHT, with shoulder joint at BOTTOM LEFT of that quarter, slightly narrower to suggest the farther wing in a side-on flying crane. Keep all three pieces visually separated by generous transparent gaps, do not overlap any parts, no extra pieces. The pieces must look like parts of the same naturalistic red-crowned crane. No text, no labels, no outlines boxes, no ground, no scenery, no shadow, no paper background, no additional animals. Genuinely transparent alpha background, preserve all fine soft feather edges.

Browser QA additionally checks wheel scrolling, forward/reverse slider scrubbing, hold behavior, navigation, and desktop/mobile layouts.
