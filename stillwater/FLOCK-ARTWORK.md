## wetlands

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: a single full-bleed panoramic painted landscape background for a crane-flight website. Wide 16:9 landscape composition.
Style and medium: Neo-traditional Japanese SUMI-E and loose transparent watercolor on pale warm ivory washi. Modern elegant picture scroll aesthetic. EXPRESSIVE BROAD BRUSH WASHES AND CALLIGRAPHIC INK MARKS, simple shapes, soft bleeding pigment, generous air, restrained spare detail. NOT detailed engraving, NOT dense foliage or microscopic tiny lines, NOT realism, NOT 3D. Organic confident hand-painted brush strokes. Naturalistic landforms simplified poetically. Water celadon and pale turquoise, pine trees deep smoky jade, mountains pale blue-gray, paper ivory, small vermilion and ochre accents.
Camera/composition: FLYING above a river at bird height, looking FORWARD AND SLIGHTLY DOWN through a broad open valley. Natural perspective, vanishing point near 58% width and 36% height. Horizon has upright mountains, absolutely NOT an overhead map. The river runs from broad foreground bottom toward the center distance. Layered banks at left and right frame a clear flight corridor. Foreground brushwork larger, middle slopes smaller, distant peaks dissolve into pale mist. Upper quarter open luminous paper and wisps of pale cloud, but no vast empty sky. Full scenic painting, no birds (the flock will be rendered separately), no animals, no text, no lettering, no borders or UI, no sun disc.
Scene: THE LOTUS WETLANDS. A wide winding pale celadon river with simple floating lotus leaves and tiny pale blossoms, softly brushed reed islands, low rounded hills and occasional rocks, asymmetrical loose pine clusters. A small vermilion torii on the far right bank. Pale ink mountains layered in the distance. Quiet spring morning, serene and airy.
